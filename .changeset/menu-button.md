Add `MenuButton` (Astro) and `<lx-menu-button>` (Marko): a button that opens an accessible menu, following the WAI-ARIA menu button pattern. Items can be plain actions or links, checkboxes or radios, and can carry a badge count and a prefix. Built for filters such as Condition. Styles are `@luxoticars/ui/styles/menu-button.css` (Astro imports them itself; the Marko tag has its own).
