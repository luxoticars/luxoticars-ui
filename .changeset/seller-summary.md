Add `SellerSummary` (`@luxoticars/ui/astro/ui/SellerSummary`), an Astro component that says who is selling: the dealer's name, photo, the Luxoticars blue tick, the identity check, location, cars for sale, registration number and address. It has two sizes: `full`, a labelled card with the facts as a description list and a link to the dealer's page, and `compact`, one line for beside a price or a title. It shows only what it is given, so there is no rating or review count until the site collects them. Its stylesheet is also exported as `@luxoticars/ui/styles/seller-summary.css`.
