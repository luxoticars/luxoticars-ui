New Astro `SectionHead`: a section title with an optional link beside it (`linkHref` + `linkLabel`, such as "View all"), and a `dark` tone for the neutral background. The default slot takes anything else that sits beside the title, such as an intro line. Its stylesheet is also exported as `styles/section-head.css`. Astro only; no HTML builder or Marko tag.
